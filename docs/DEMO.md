# Feral — 90-second recording script

Use two real phone players for a clean shot, not the 300 simulated test pets. Record the host screen and one phone screen separately, then cut between them. Keep the host timer visible during gameplay. The final video has 20 seconds of introduction, a real 60-second game, and 10 seconds for the result. Trim setup and the model’s initial hiding wait; do not speed up the countdown or pretend a prepared shot happened live.

## Prepare before recording

1. On the host laptop, open [Feral](https://feral-hackathon.sugarybrine.chatgpt.site) and click **Create room**. Use the published Site for the recording; no local server is needed.
2. Give both phones and the laptop internet access, then scan the host’s QR code on each phone. They do not need the same Wi-Fi. Verify joining before the take. For an offline LAN rehearsal only, run `npm run dev`, use `http://localhost:4173`, and connect all devices to the same Wi-Fi.
3. On phone A, swipe to **Dewey**, then press **Join game →**. Capture a short recording of the swipe and join for the opening sequence.
4. On phone B, describe a custom pet, for example: “Sir Tokens, a tiny owl with brass goggles and oversized wings.” Give it that name and press **Join game →**. Let card generation finish before the take. For the quickest rehearsal, choose **Fireball** instead; built-in cards are presets, so do not describe them as newly generated by AI.
5. Wait until the custom pet appears in the host’s castle and on its phone. The host now keeps only the QR code in place of the old menu; do not plan a Petdex menu shot. Custom generation latency varies.
6. Rehearse the camera: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Locate the two small care buildings in the garden. Click **Look inside** once to locate Tibo’s portrait and the red reset button in the front-left tavern; return to the wide view for the opening.
7. Assign roles: one host/camera operator, phone A driver, phone B driver, and optionally a narrator. After the pets merge, only phone A should steer; both phones control the same new pet, so simultaneous inputs compete.
8. Start screen recording. Leave the host on the lobby with both players connected. Do not use **Stop** during the timed take: it correctly freezes the game and would extend a continuous recording beyond 90 seconds. Use it only for rehearsal or between takes.

## The 90-second cut

Times below are video timestamps. The game timer first shows a running **1:00 at 00:20**. Press **Start** beneath the large timer and trim any “Todd is hiding…” wait immediately before that point. The model chooses the hiding room before the countdown starts.

| Video time | Exact action and shot | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **00:00–00:08** | Wide host shot: slowly orbit the whole mansion and illuminated gardens. | “This is Feral: your AI pet, a haunted castle, and one minute to survive.” |
| **00:08–00:15** | Cut to the recorded phone shot: scan the QR code, swipe to Dewey, and join. End on the arrow controller. | “Scan the code, choose a Codex companion, or bring your own pet.” |
| **00:15–00:20** | Show the custom pet briefly, if prepared. Cut back to the host and the **Start** click. Trim the hiding wait. | “Custom pets get balanced AI-generated abilities. Find the hidden Dot, or finish with the most usage.” |
| **00:20–00:26** | The real countdown begins. On phone A hold **→** for about one second and release. On phone B hold **←** briefly and release. Show the two pets moving on the host, with a small phone inset. | “Your phone moves your pet in the same live world. The server enforces the rules.” |
| **00:26–00:48** | Both players tap **Mating room**, then release all arrows. The pets follow the paths automatically. Frame the nursery on the host. Hold the shot through arrival, the rising hearts and the eight-second duvet animation. End on the new pet and its combined name on both phones. | “Only two can enter. Once both arrive, they merge into one new pet—and both players share control.” |
| **00:48–00:58** | After the merge, phone A taps **Go to vet**. Keep the nursery and vet in frame while the pet travels. Show the phone’s usage increasing on arrival. | “Usage is your health. The vet restores it; hidden bottles drain it and make your pet tipsy.” |
| **00:58–01:10** | Leave both phones’ arrows released while the host tours the mansion. Click **Look inside** if the roof has not opened automatically, then frame the room details and Tibo’s portrait/reset. This is a visual tour: do not claim the reset was collected unless the pet actually enters the tavern. | “Every room has its own lore and access rules. Tibo’s reset gives the first visitor a full recharge.” |
| **01:10–01:20** | Pull back to show the shared pet and the final ten seconds. Release movement if needed so the result is easy to follow. Keep the countdown continuous to **0:00**. | “The Dot is still hidden. If nobody finds it, the most usage at the bell wins. Exact ties share the win.” |
| **01:20–01:30** | Hold on the actual winner result, then end with the castle and a simple **Feral** title added in the video editor. | “One castle. One minute. Feral.” |

The room’s actual travel and merge timing takes precedence over a shot boundary. If the merge takes longer than the rehearsal, borrow seconds from the room tour; do not cut away before the new pet appears. The care shortcuts work automatically, but holding an arrow cancels that route.

## If someone finds the Dot early

The game ends immediately; keep that real reveal. Say: **“They found the hidden Dot—instant win.”** Use the remaining video time for a clearly separate castle tour and the closing title. Do not show a later `0:00` result as if it belongs to that same game, reveal the Dot’s location in advance, or claim a staged discovery.

The normal take above prioritizes the merge, recovery and full-minute ending. Finding the randomly placed Dot is a bonus, not a dependable recording cue.

## If something goes wrong during a take

- **Need time to explain?** The host clicks **Stop** beneath the timer. **Resume** continues from the same time, including movement, usage and breeding. Treat the pause as a retake or edit around it; do not call it a continuous 60-second round.
- **Need a fresh game?** When the result appears, the host clicks **Play again**. The original pets return and players stay connected. Start a new take from the lobby.
- **Phone loses its connection?** Reload its existing game tab. It reconnects to that pet and the current timer; it does not start a new game.
- **A model request falls back?** Keep the demo factual. Preset pets and movement work without a model call; do not call an offline fallback an AI-generated result.

## Claims to use accurately

- “Up to 300 players” is the room limit. Earlier Node/Durable Object tests connected **300 simulated player WebSockets plus a host**. Sites uses HTTP polling; use its separate measurements in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`, and do not present either test as 300 physical phones.
- AI generates custom cards and chooses the initial hiding room. Movement, access gates, health, merging and winners are enforced by game code. The model’s hidden chain of thought is not displayed.
- This is a multiplayer website with a big-screen host and phone controllers. It is publicly deployed at https://feral-hackathon.sugarybrine.chatgpt.site. Players use a game session, without ChatGPT account login.
- Built-in sprites come from Max’s companion update. Credit Max and Owen in the video description alongside the repository link.

Hosted recording uses https://feral-hackathon.sugarybrine.chatgpt.site. Phone arrows remain locked until the host presses Start, then both screens count down together. The phone submission button is **Join game →**.

For the traitor version, prejoin nine players before recording. One phone privately reveals its traitor role at Start. Outside base camp, steer that pet into a seeker: the seeker moves to the graveyard and its phone shows **Banned for distillation — your pet is dead**. Use this beat instead of part of the room tour; keep the same 60-second hunt and highest-usage ending.
